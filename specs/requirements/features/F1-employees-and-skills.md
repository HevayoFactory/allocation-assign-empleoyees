# Employees &amp; skills

## Purpose

Bring in the employee directory from the HR system and maintain each employee's skills, so staffing can match people to needs.

## User Stories

- F1.1 As a resource manager, I browse and search employees imported from the HR system by name, role and department.
- F1.2 As a resource manager, I filter employees by skill and proficiency level.
- F1.3 As an administrator, I maintain the list of skills and proficiency levels.
- F1.4 As an administrator, I assign skills with a proficiency level to an employee.
- F1.5 As an employee, I view my own profile and skills.
- F1.6 As an administrator, I see when employee data was last synchronised from the HR system and can trigger a refresh.

## Decisions

- Employees are taken from the HR system, not entered in this app.
- Employee records are synchronised from the HR system and are read-only here; only skills are maintained in this app.
