# Availability &amp; conflicts

## Purpose

Show who is available when and flag over-allocation or other conflicts, for resource managers choosing who to assign.

Needs: F3.

## User Stories

- F4.1 As a resource manager, I see each employee's total allocated load over time on a timeline.
- F4.2 As a resource manager, I find employees available for a date range and a minimum free load.
- F4.3 As a resource manager, I am warned when an allocation takes an employee above 100% in any period.
- F4.4 As a resource manager, I see a list of all currently over-allocated employees.
- F4.5 As a resource manager, I see employees who are unallocated (on the bench).

## Decisions

- Over-allocation is flagged, never blocked.
