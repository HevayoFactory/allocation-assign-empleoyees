# My assignments

## Purpose

Let an employee see their own current and upcoming assignments.

Needs: F3.

## User Stories

- F5.1 As an employee, I see my current and upcoming assignments with engagement, dates and load.
- F5.2 As an employee, I see my past assignments.
- F5.3 As an employee, I see my free capacity in the coming weeks.

## Decisions

- An employee sees only their own assignments.
